How To Use SongGen

Everything you need to know about creating AI music.

1

Sign in with Google

Click "Continue with Google" on the landing page. We create your personal library automatically — no passwords, no setup.

2

Write a prompt

Go to Generate and describe your track. The more detail you give — mood, instruments, energy, theme — the better the result.

Example prompts:

  • "An upbeat summer pop song about road trips, catchy guitar, sing-along chorus"
  • "A melancholic jazz ballad about rainy city nights, saxophone, slow tempo"
  • "Energetic hip-hop track about chasing dreams, heavy bass, confident rapper"
3

Wait for generation

In mock mode (default), results are instant. With the real Suno API, it typically takes 20–60 seconds. The page polls automatically — you don't need to refresh.

4

Play, share, or regenerate

Your song is saved to your library. Open it to listen, share publicly with a unique link, or regenerate with a tweaked prompt.

⚙️ Under the Hood: Strategy Pattern

SongGen uses the Strategy design pattern to swap AI providers without changing any other code. Two strategies are available:

🔧 Mock Strategy

Instant offline generation. No API key needed. Perfect for development and testing.

GENERATOR_STRATEGY=mock

🎵 Suno Strategy

Real AI generation via the Suno API. Requires an API key from sunoapi.org.

GENERATOR_STRATEGY=suno

Switch strategies by changing one line in your .env file.
